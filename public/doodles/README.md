# Doodle images

The doodle SVGs in this folder are from **Twemoji** by Twitter.

- Source: https://github.com/twitter/twemoji
- License: CC-BY 4.0

`dentist.png` comes from rawpixel via Openverse and is CC0 1.0.

They are used as task illustrations in ViDo. The text of each task remains available through the accessible label and the hover title.

`run.svg` is based on the Tabler Icons treadmill icon (MIT).

`heatpump.png` and `washer.png` come from Wikimedia Commons and are CC0.

`laundry-pro.jpg` is a CC0 laundromat photo from Flickr via Openverse.

Laundry and dishes are composed from the Twemoji basket plus clothes or plates/cups.

Sporting images (`walking`, `cricket`, `football`, `basketball`, `tennis`, `badminton`, `volleyball`, `swimming`, `cycling`, `hockey`, `baseball`, `tabletennis`, `boxing`, `golf`, and `sport`) are Twemoji under the same CC-BY 4.0 licence.

Writing tasks are composed in the app from `memo.svg` and `pencil.svg`, so they show paper and a pencil.

The keyword-to-image catalog lives in `src/features/workspace/doodles/taskImageCatalog.ts`.
